A Visit to the Botanical Garden

Last Saturday, our family visited a botanical garden outside the city. The weather was pleasant, and the walking paths were surrounded by flowering plants and tall trees. A guide explained how the garden protects rare species and provides a home for butterflies and birds.

We stopped near a small pond to watch the fish before having lunch beneath a shaded pavilion. On the way out, we bought a few packets of seeds for our balcony. The visit reminded us that even a small green space can make a busy city feel calmer.
